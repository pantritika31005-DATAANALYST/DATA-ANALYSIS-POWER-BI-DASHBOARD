# AI and Student Life Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview
Developed an interactive **AI and Student Life Analysis Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI** to explore students' use of AI tools, academic applications, career confidence, time-saving benefits, and ethical concerns. The dashboard transforms student survey data into visual insights to understand how AI adoption relates to students' academic experiences and perceptions.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Average Career Confidence:** 5.42
- **High-Confidence Students:** 3
- **Most-Used AI Tool:** GitHub Copilot
- **Most Common AI Use Case:** Exam Preparation
- **High Ethics Concern:** 496

## Visualizations and Analysis

### 1. Student Concerns About AI Ethics – Doughnut Chart
Displays the distribution of students' ethical concerns about AI across three categories:
- Low
- Medium
- High

This visualization helps compare the prevalence of different concern levels regarding responsible AI use.

### 2. Where AI Saves Students the Most Time – Horizontal Bar Chart
Compares AI's perceived time-saving benefits across five academic activities:
- Brainstorming
- Code Debugging
- Essay Drafting
- Exam Preparation
- Literature Review

The chart highlights differences in reported time savings across common student tasks.

### 3. AI Usage and Career Confidence – Column Chart
Compares career confidence across three categories:
- Medium (4–7)
- High (8–10)
- Low (1–3)

The displayed values are approximately 3.0K for Medium and 2.6K each for High and Low. These figures should be validated against the underlying data and aggregation method before interpreting them as student counts or confidence averages.

### 4. AI Ethics Concern and Career Confidence – Stacked Column Chart
Compares career-confidence groups across different AI ethics concern levels. The visualization displays the distribution of High, Medium, and Low career-confidence categories for students grouped by ethics concern.

This helps explore whether students with different levels of concern about AI ethics also report different levels of career confidence.

## Key Insights
- **AI tool adoption:** GitHub Copilot is identified as the most-used AI tool in the displayed data.
- **Academic applications:** Exam preparation is highlighted as the most common AI use case.
- **Time-saving opportunities:** AI is reported to save time across brainstorming, coding, essay writing, exam preparation, and literature review.
- **Ethical awareness:** The dashboard tracks low, medium, and high levels of concern about AI ethics, helping identify areas for responsible AI education.
- **Career confidence:** The dashboard compares career-confidence groups to explore differences in students' perceptions of their future careers.
- **Ethics and confidence:** The stacked chart enables comparisons between ethical concerns and career-confidence levels without assuming that one causes the other.

## Interactive Features
- Interactive chart filtering and cross-highlighting.
- Visual comparisons across AI ethics concern and career-confidence categories.
- Dashboard navigation buttons and filter controls.

# DMart Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze DMart's sales performance and customer behavior. This project provides insights into total sales, average order value, monthly sales trends, product performance, category-wise sales, subscription patterns, customer demographics, and payment methods.

## Key Features
- **Sales Overview:** Total sales, average order value, average website time, and total orders.
- **Customer Insights:** Subscription-based order value, website engagement by age group, and gender-wise sales analysis.
- **Product Insights:** Product performance and discount analysis.
- **Interactive Visualizations:** Charts, slicers, and navigation buttons for exploring business data.
- **Business Intelligence:** Data-driven insights to support customer understanding and sales performance analysis.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Data Visualization
- Data Analysis

## Objective
To transform raw retail data into meaningful visual insights, identify customer and product trends, and support data-driven business decisions.
